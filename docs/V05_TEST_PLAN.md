# FieldSec v0.5 Physical Validation Plan

This release has static/package validation only in the current build environment. Before calling v0.5 device-validated, run the following on the official Flipper Zero and official Wi-Fi Developer Board.

## 1. Build and launch
- Build the FAP with current uFBT against official firmware.
- Confirm application loads without API mismatch or crash.
- Confirm v0.4 state files migrate without loss of engagement, target, settings or Academy status.

## 2. UART Workbench
- Connect Flipper RX to a known 3.3 V UART source and common ground.
- Verify TX remains electrically inactive with a logic analyzer.
- Test 9600 and 115200 baud sources.
- Confirm text and hex previews match captured bytes.
- Confirm printable ratio is sensible for ASCII and binary test streams.
- Confirm `uart_capture.bin` and `uart_history.csv` are written.
- Confirm Expansion service returns after capture and after failure paths.

## 3. I2C Workbench
- Attach a known 3.3 V I2C peripheral.
- Confirm its 7-bit address is reported.
- Add/remove one peripheral between runs and verify new/missing counts.
- Confirm no writes are emitted by observing SDA/SCL with a logic analyzer.

## 4. Wi-Fi Workbench
- Flash the v0.5 companion firmware to the official Developer Board.
- Verify passive scanning with a separate monitor-mode capture: no directed/active probe requests should be generated by the FieldSec scan configuration.
- Run two surveys with a controlled AP added/removed between runs.
- Verify new/disappeared BSSID counts and `+` annotation.
- Confirm raw `wifi_scan.txt` is retained.

## 5. Target Intelligence
- Create at least two targets.
- Populate all profile fields.
- Reboot and verify persistence and correct profile association.
- Confirm tabs/newlines entered through text fields are sanitized in storage.

## 6. Structured Findings
- Create a finding with all fields.
- Confirm CSV row has 10 columns and status `Open`.
- Generate a desktop Markdown report and verify field placement.
- Test a legacy v0.4 five-column finding for compatibility.

## 7. Training Academy
- Open a paired lesson, mark it in progress, then attempt completion without practice; verify completion is blocked.
- Launch the paired instrument, return, and complete the lesson.
- Reboot and verify practice counts and statuses persist.

## 8. Failure recovery
- Remove or write-protect the microSD card and verify failures are reported without crash.
- Force USART contention and verify FieldSec exits the workbench cleanly and restores Expansion ownership.
