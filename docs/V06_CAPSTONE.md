# FieldSec v0.6 Capstone: Authorized IoT Assessment

The capstone is designed to teach the complete assessment lifecycle with the same FieldSec records used in field operation.

## Scenario

Use an IoT device, development board, intentionally vulnerable target, or other hardware you own or are authorized to assess.

## Required workflow

1. Create and name the engagement.
2. Record a clear scope note and acknowledge authorization.
3. Create/select the target and populate available target intelligence.
4. Form a testable hypothesis before using a workbench.
5. Gather at least three observations with `OBS-######` IDs.
6. Separate direct observation from inference.
7. Create at least one structured finding and cite the supporting observation IDs.
8. State a defensive control and objective retest criterion.
9. Apply or simulate the authorized remediation in the lab.
10. Record a retest linked to the `FND-######` identifier.
11. Export the dataset and generate the desktop report.
12. Explain which conclusions are proven, which remain hypotheses, and what least-invasive test would resolve remaining uncertainty.

## Instructor prompts

- What fact is directly demonstrated by each observation?
- What alternative benign explanation exists?
- Which evidence IDs support the finding?
- Does the impact exceed what the evidence actually proves?
- Is the remediation specific to the demonstrated weakness?
- Would another analyst understand exactly what constitutes a successful retest?

## Passing standard

A complete capstone is not defined by finding a vulnerability. A defensible result may be "no validated weakness found" if the student demonstrates correct scoping, evidence handling, analysis, and retesting discipline.
